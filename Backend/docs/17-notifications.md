# Module 17 — Notifications & Alerts

## Objective
Provide centralized notifications for important platform events.

## Events
- Account verification.
- Architect verification.
- Subscription expiration.
- Payment confirmation.
- AI generation completion.
- Material estimation completion.
- New message.
- Project activity.
- Order status.
- Product availability.
- New review.

## Channels
- In-app
- Email
- Push notifications
- SMS as a future option

## Requirements
- Read/unread state.
- Notification preferences.
- Event-based generation.
- Secure user targeting.
