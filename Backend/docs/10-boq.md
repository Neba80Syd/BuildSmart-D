# Module 10 — Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

## Objective
Generate and manage a preliminary Bill of Quantities from estimated materials.

## Functional Requirements
- Generate BOQ.
- Add/edit/remove items.
- Store quantity and unit.
- Add estimated unit prices.
- Calculate totals.
- Calculate project cost estimate.
- Export/share BOQ.
- Maintain versions.

## Data Example
Material | Quantity | Unit | Unit Price | Total

## Dependencies
- Material Estimation
- Project Management
- Marketplace
