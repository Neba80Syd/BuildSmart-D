# Module 21 — Analytics & Reporting

## Objective
Provide useful operational and business insights.

## Admin Metrics
- Total users.
- Active users.
- Architects.
- Vendors.
- Projects.
- AI generations.
- Revenue.
- Orders.
- Marketplace sales.

## Architect Metrics
- Projects.
- Clients.
- Designs generated.
- Completed projects.
- Ratings.
- Revenue.

## Vendor Metrics
- Products.
- Orders.
- Revenue.
- Inventory.
- Best sellers.

## Requirements
- Role-specific dashboards.
- Date filters.
- Aggregations.
- Exportable reports where appropriate.
